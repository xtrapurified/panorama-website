Studio

Panorama Films is an independent creative storytelling studio working across documentaries, series, campaigns and brand films.

We explore science, technology, culture and the human stories around them, with commissioned films for global brands, NGOs and institutions alongside our own projects.

The future, documented.

Creative Director

Andrija Kovač

Co-founder of Panorama Films Collective and director of photography, exploring the space between film, photography and design. He has directed and shot short films, video essays and branded films for companies, NGOs, startups, artists and activist groups.

Producer

Mina Strugar

Creative producer and audio engineer, leading projects and creative development. She has created museum experiences and worked on theatre productions. Her research focuses on spatial sound and sound design.

Editorial Director

Marija Kovačina

Film editor, multimedia artist and musician. She has edited and directed documentaries, art films and short experimental works, screened and awarded at international festivals.

Collective

An open circle of cinematographers, editors, sound designers and producers. Each film brings a different part of it together.

    Approach

    We stay with a film from the first question to the final frame: the research and the interviews, the script, the shoot and the cut, so nothing gets lost between hands.

    Manifesto
    I

    Impact

    We tell the stories shaping tomorrow’s society. From education and social activism to the movements driving change, we partner with foundations, NGOs and local innovators to spotlight the people and ideas working toward a better future.

    II

    Innovation

    We explore how technology reshapes our lives. From VR to drones, to artificial intelligence and new platforms, we work with startups, researchers and visionaries to tell stories of innovation that serve the greater good.

    III

    Culture

    Culture is a story in motion. We work with artists, musicians and creators who push boundaries, capturing the energy of emerging trends and making visible the ideas shaping tomorrow’s art, music and fashion.

    IV

    Expression

    We celebrate those who push boundaries. Guided by athletes, explorers and outdoor pioneers, we bring to light new ways of living, moving and experiencing the world.
