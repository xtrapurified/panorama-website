One studio, first question to final frame.

We stay with a film from the first question to the final frame: the research and the interviews, the script, the shoot and the cut, so nothing gets lost between hands.

Our work in the Emirates spans the Dubai Future Forum, the docuseries What Does the Future Hold? for the Dubai Future Foundation, Crew-6 astronauts at the Museum of the Future, the World Government Summit, EXPO 2020 Dubai, the World Sports Summit and Dubai Future Week. We work with a small core team in Belgrade and a crew of cinematographers, editors and sound designers who travel wherever the film is.

Services: concept, script, creative direction, direction, production, cinematography, aerial cinematography, editing, sound production, animation and design.
