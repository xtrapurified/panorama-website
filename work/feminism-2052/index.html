Feminism 2052

Client Center for Women’s Studies SerbiaYear 2022Services Direction, cinematography, editing

Centre for Women's Studies in Serbia was initiated by the feminist activist group "Woman and Society". Its first experimental one-semester course started symbolically on March 8, 1992. In 2022, Center celebrates it’s 30th birthday with a 4 part mini-series tackling subjects like the future of feminism, women’s rights, activism and women’s studies. Produced by Nađa Gluhaček
