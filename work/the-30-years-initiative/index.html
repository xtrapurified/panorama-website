The 30 Years Initiative

Client Open Society FoundationsYear 2021Services Direction, service production, cinematography, editing

Service for Open Society Foundations

The 30 Years is an initiative that provides perspective on three decades (1991–2021) of the presence and work of the Open Society Foundations (OSF) in the Balkans (Albania, Bosnia and Herzegovina, Croatia, Kosovo, Montenegro, North Macedonia, Serbia and Slovenia). It examines how the unique philanthropic efforts, open society ideas and philosophy persevered during the stormy recent history of the region and how the work is still a moving force, alive and active. The initiative also looks toward the future—the many challenges and opportunities facing the region as each country finds its place within today’s Europe. * Cover photo courtesy of Ms. Goranka Matić www.opensocietyfoundations.org/
