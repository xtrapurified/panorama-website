World Government Summit

Client World Government SummitYear 2017–2019, 2022–2023Services Creative direction, production, cinematography, editing

The World Government Summit is a global platform dedicated to shaping the future of government worldwide. Each year, the Summit sets the agenda for the next generation of governments with a focus on how they can harness innovation and technology to solve universal challenges facing humanity. Commissioned by the WGS these films are the official documents showcasing the event.
